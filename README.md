# Data Careers Survey

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing career paths, salaries, skills, accessibility, and growth opportunities across the data field.

## 📌 Project Background

A university conducted a survey to understand the growing data field and evaluate whether introducing a dedicated Data Analytics department could provide meaningful career opportunities for students.

I analyzed the survey from a **career and employability perspective**, focusing on what different data roles offer in terms of accessibility, salary, skills, career growth, satisfaction, and stability.

## 🎯 Objective

To turn survey responses into actionable insights that can help:

- The university understand the career potential within the data field.
- Students identify suitable entry-level and long-term career paths.
- Career switchers understand the skills required to transition into the field.

## ❓ Key Business Questions

- Which data roles have the strongest workforce presence?
- Which roles offer the highest salary potential?
- How accessible is the data field for freshers and career switchers?
- What education and skills are common across data careers?
- Which skills create mobility across roles and industries?
- Which roles offer stronger growth, stability, satisfaction, and work-life balance?
- What career pathways can be recommended based on the findings?

## 📊 Dashboard

The dashboard explores four major areas:

**Market Value**  
Workforce composition, salaries, industries, and career-switching patterns.

**Future Outcome**  
Age distribution, education, career priorities, and perceived entry difficulty.

**Skills & Opportunities**  
Programming languages, skills across industries, and role-specific requirements.

**Growth & Stability**  
Career quality, salary satisfaction, work-life balance, happiness, and upward mobility.

A dedicated **Insights & Recommendations** page translates the analysis into practical career guidance.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Data Analyst** emerges as a practical entry-level pathway due to its strong workforce presence and relative accessibility.
- **Data Scientist** shows the strongest salary potential among the surveyed roles, highlighting the value of deeper technical specialization.
- **Python and other technical skills** appear across multiple roles and industries, making them valuable transferable skills.
- **Salary alone does not define career quality** — work-life balance, satisfaction, growth, and stability also vary across roles.
- The data field offers **multiple career pathways**, allowing professionals to progress from foundational roles toward more specialized positions.

## 💡 Recommendations

**For Freshers**  
Build foundational skills in SQL, Python, Excel, statistics, and data visualization. Data Analyst can serve as a practical entry point before specializing further.

**For Career Switchers**  
Combine existing domain knowledge with analytical and technical skills to transition into data-related roles.

**For Long-Term Growth**  
Use entry-level experience as a foundation for progressing toward specialized roles such as Data Scientist, Data Engineer, or Data Architect.

**For the University**  
The breadth of career opportunities identified in the survey supports exploring a dedicated data-focused program with an emphasis on practical, industry-relevant skills.
